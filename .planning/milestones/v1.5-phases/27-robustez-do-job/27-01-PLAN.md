<objective>
Close JOB-02: `apollo rotina template criar/editar --tipo-geracao du_fixo --offset-dias` currently rejects
any value below 1, so the real onboarding calendar's two "Prévia DU-2" templates (offset -2 business days
from month end) could not be represented at all. This plan extends `du_fixo` to accept `offsetDias <= 0`
with the semantics `0 = last business day of the month`, `negative N = N business days before it`, built on
a new `nth_business_day_from_month_end` primitive in `bizdays.py`/`.ts` that reuses the existing
`add_business_days` (which already supports a negative step count) rather than duplicating calendar
navigation. `du_fixo`'s forward-counting behavior for `offsetDias >= 1` (via the pre-existing
`nth_business_day_of_month`) is completely unchanged; `corrido_fixo` is untouched entirely (CONTEXT.md
decision 2 — the original finding is specifically about business days, not calendar days).

Purpose: the RBR onboarding's real "Prévia DU-2" templates become representable, without duplicating the CLI/
test/fixture surface with a new `tipoGeracao` (offset<=0 remains semantically "the Nth business day of the
month", just counted from the other end).
Output: `nth_business_day_from_month_end` in both runtimes, `du_fixo`'s validation/dispatch updated in both
runtimes, corrected `--offset-dias` help text, dedicated low-level and scenario-level fixture coverage, and a
new permanent live test proving the exact real onboarding case (2026-08-27 for August 2026, offset -2)
end-to-end against the production InstantDB app.
</objective>
