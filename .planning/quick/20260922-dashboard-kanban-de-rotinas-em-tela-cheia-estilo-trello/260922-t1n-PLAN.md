<objective>
Redesign the Dashboard's routines kanban (`RoutinesByFundo.svelte`, the fundo-grouped "kanban de rotinas" shipped in Phase 22 — its own directory is literally named `22-dashboard-kanbans-rotinas-heatmap`) from a short, content-hugging vertical stack capped at 4 items per fundo into a full-height, internally-scrollable, Trello-style board: each fundo becomes a fixed-width column (mirroring `ProjectStrips.svelte`'s already-proven horizontal-kanban-column pattern), the column body scrolls internally instead of truncating at 4 items with a "+N" label, card titles wrap onto up to 2 lines instead of 1, and each card additionally shows its linked fundo's name.

Purpose: today the routines kanban shows only ~3-4 items per fundo (hard `.slice(0, 4)` cap) with single-line, effectively-truncated titles, and leaves the tall grid cell CSS Grid already allocates it (`lg:row-span-2`, matching WeekCalendar + ProjectStrips' combined height) mostly empty below a short block — a real usability gap once an account has many routines in a week.
Output: `RoutinesByFundo.svelte` renders every fundo's full, unfiltered/uncapped this-week routine list inside its own scrollable column; `Dashboard.svelte`'s wrapping layout lets that column genuinely consume the height its grid cell already receives; `dashboard.spec.ts`'s DASH-04 fixture proves the new no-cap/scroll/2-line/fundo-name behavior live against the real InstantDB app.
</objective>
